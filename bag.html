Your bag

Your bag lives in this browser and nowhere else until you check out. We do not hold it on our servers, we do not email you about it later, and we do not use it to follow you around the internet. Leave it sitting for a week if you want to.

Items

An empty bag is a fine place to be. We keep 30 styles in the line and 22 of them are seasonless, so nothing here is going anywhere in a hurry. If a size has sold through, it comes back in the next volume rather than being rushed out early.

Before you check out

  • Sizes are in inches, not guesses. The size guide lists body measurements for every style, and tells you which way to go if you are between two
  • One more item can cost you less. Anything over $125 ships free, so a pair of socks below the line is often cheaper than the $8 flat rate
  • Slow Line pieces move at their own pace. They are cut to order on an 18 to 24 business day lead time and exchange for another size rather than refund

Payment is processed by our PCI-compliant payment provider. We never see or store your card number.

Using your email for checkout does not subscribe you to anything. Marketing email is a separate opt-in with a one-click unsubscribe, as described in our Privacy Policy.

What happens at checkout

Payment

  • Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay
  • Card details go straight to our payment processor over TLS. They never reach our servers and we never see them
  • Gift cards and referral credit can be combined with any payment method

Taxes and duties

  • US sales tax is calculated at checkout from your shipping address
  • We collect sales tax in the states where we have an obligation to, and in no others
  • International orders ship without US tax. Duties and import charges are settled by whoever receives the parcel
  • Nothing is added to the total after you confirm the order

After you order

  • An order confirmation arrives immediately with an order number
  • A tracking number follows within one business day
  • Slow Line pieces carry an estimated ship date, currently 18 to 24 business days, because they are cut after you order
  • Any order that has not shipped can be changed or cancelled. Write to us and a person will answer

The number you see is the number you pay

Nothing is added to your bag on your behalf. There is no pre-ticked subscription box, no countdown pretending to hold your size, and no handling fee that surfaces on the last screen. If the total moves between this page and the payment step, it moved for one of two reasons: the shipping service you picked, or sales tax. Both are shown to you first.

If something is still unclear

Not sure on size

  • Body measurements in inches for every style in the line
  • A plain recommendation for each piece when you fall between two sizes
  • Or send us your measurements and we will name a size rather than hedge
  • Open the size guide

Getting it to you

  • Rates and timings for every US service, including Alaska, Hawaii and the territories
  • International destinations and how duties are handled
  • What we do when a parcel goes missing, which is replace it
  • Open shipping and delivery

If it is not right

  • 30 days to send it back, unworn with the tags still on
  • Size exchanges ship free both ways, once per order
  • Refund returns carry a $7 label fee, which is what the label costs us
  • Open returns and exchanges
