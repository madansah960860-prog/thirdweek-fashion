Seasonless by design

The kit that never changes

Two volumes come out each year and none of them touch this page much. Of the 30 styles in the line, 22 are seasonless, and most of those 22 are small. Here is what stays in the bag all year, what gets added when the light goes, and what only comes out for the long days.

In the bag all year

  • Ridgeline Field Cap: sun in July, rain off the face in November, folds flat between
  • Grip Crew Socks: three pairs means one on, one in the wash, one clean
  • The headband: the cheapest thing on this page and the one people re-order most
  • Packable Musette: 3.4 oz, so there is no argument for leaving it out

Added when the light goes

  • Coldstart Merino Beanie: single layer, 1.7 oz, fits under a hood
  • Contact Point Gloves: the first cold thing you want and the last you put away
  • Rule of thumb: hands and ears first, another body layer second
  • Both wash the same way: cold, flat dry, never near a radiator

Out for the long days

  • The hydration belt: two flasks, a phone and food, carried flat at the waist
  • The 32 liter duffel: a change of kit, shoes and a laptop, with the wet end sealed off
  • Flasks: sold separately, standard 17 oz soft flasks fit the sleeves
  • After a salty day: rinse the belt in cold water and hang it, or it goes stiff

Why none of this is redesigned every spring

A cap that fits your head is worth more than a new color. We keep these shapes still so a replacement matches the one you have been wearing for three years, and so a size you learned once stays true. When something does change here it is because a part failed in wear testing, not because a season turned. The duffel is the exception that proves the point: its zips and straps carry free repair for three years, because those are the parts that go first and the rest of the bag outlives them easily.

How we decide what stays

These make good gifts

Caps, socks and beanies are what people buy for other people, because they fit almost everyone and they get used rather than admired. If you are unsure of a size, a gift card takes the guesswork out. They run from $25 to $500 and they do not expire.
