Three routes

How to reach the right person

The inbox is small enough that nothing gets lost in it, but sending a note to the right desk first still saves a day of forwarding. These are the three routes, and what each one owes you.

Orders, sizing and fit

  • Write to: help@haldenfield.com
  • Answered in: One business day, and most before lunch
  • Tracking, labels, exchanges, and anything with an order number attached to it
  • Call (828) 555-0164 if you would rather settle a size in one conversation

Wholesale and press

  • Write to: wholesale@haldenfield.com or press@haldenfield.com
  • Answered in: Two business days, with a line sheet attached
  • Shop applications, price lists, sample loans and image requests
  • The terms are published on the stockists page, so there is nothing to negotiate first

Privacy and data

  • Write to: privacy@haldenfield.com
  • Answered in: One business day to acknowledge, then on the timelines set out in our policy
  • Access, correction and deletion requests, and opting out of sale or sharing
  • The full detail sits in the Privacy Policy

If you are not sure which one

Send it to hello@haldenfield.com and we will carry it to the right desk ourselves. Nothing comes back to you asking for a different address.

Read the FAQ first

What happens after you write

Sizing questions

  • Give us chest, waist and height in inches, and name the style
  • We name one size back, and describe how it will sit at the shoulder and the hem
  • If the pattern is wrong for your proportions, we say so rather than sell you one

A parcel gone quiet

  • Send the order number and we will go and look at the shelf it left from
  • Missing and damaged parcels are ours to settle, not yours to chase with the carrier
  • No photographs are needed before we send a replacement

Repairs and faults

  • A fault in the making is repaired or replaced at no cost, with no time limit on it
  • Honest wear is charged at parts cost plus return shipping, so the piece keeps going
  • Send a photograph of the damage. We quote before you post anything
