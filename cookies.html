Cookie policy

Last updated September 1, 2026. Nothing beyond strictly necessary cookies is set until you choose in the banner. This page lists every cookie the site can set, who sets it, what it does and how long it lasts.

Your current choice

You can change your cookie preferences whenever you like. Rejecting non-essential cookies closes off no part of the store, and it does not change prices or shipping.

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a website stores in your browser. Some are set by us (first-party) and some by other companies whose services we use (third-party). Similar technologies, including local storage, work the same way and are covered by this policy. Your bag, for example, is held in local storage rather than in a cookie, and is treated as strictly necessary.

Categories

Strictly necessary, always on

Required for the site to work. These do not follow you across other websites, and they cannot be switched off, because without them the bag, the checkout security and the record of your own consent all stop working.

Analytics, off until you consent

Aggregated measurement of how the site is used, so we can tell which pages help and which ones fail people. If you reject this category, no analytics identifier is created.

Advertising, off until you consent

Used to measure and personalize advertising, including cookies set by Google. If you reject this category, no advertising cookie is set and no advertising identifier is transmitted from this site.

Third-party services on this site

  • Google Fonts. Typefaces are requested from Google's font servers. This transmits your IP address to Google in order to deliver the font file. No cookie is set by this request.
  • Image hosting. Product and editorial photography is served from a third-party image host, which receives your IP address to deliver the image. No cookie is set.
  • Google Analytics and Google advertising services, only after you consent to the relevant category.

How to control cookies

  • Our banner. Accept all, reject non-essential, or open preferences and choose per category. Your choice is stored for 12 months, then we ask again.
  • Your browser. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will break the bag and checkout.
  • Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends a GPC signal, we treat it as an opt-out of advertising cookies and of "sharing" as defined by California law.
  • Google Ads Settings. Personalized advertising from Google can be turned off at adssettings.google.com.
  • Industry opt-outs. optout.aboutads.info and optout.networkadvertising.org.
