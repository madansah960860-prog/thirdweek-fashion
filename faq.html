Questions and answers

These are the questions the inbox gets week after week, written out in full rather than pointed at a policy page. If yours is not here, write to us. We will answer it, and then it will be here.

Sizing and fit

XS through XXL in every apparel style, with the US numeric equivalents set out in the size guide. Socks come in S, M and L against US shoe sizes. Gloves run S to XL. Caps, beanies and headbands are one size, with the fit range given in inches.

Patterns are graded across the whole range rather than scaled up and down from one middle size. It is slower work on the bench, and it is why XS and XXL sit the way they were drawn.

It turns on the garment rather than on you. Take the smaller size for close-cut pieces and for anything with 8% or more elastane, which recovers its shape. Take the larger for non-stretch wovens like the Longshadow Anorak, and any time a mid layer is going underneath.

The size guide carries a note for every style. If you would rather just ask, send your chest, waist and height and we will name one size back.

You. Every figure is a body measurement, in inches. Flat garment measurements exist for every style as well, and we will send them if you want to lay a piece against something already in your wardrobe. Name the style and the size and they come back the same day.

Unisex, cut on a straight block, with articulated sleeves and hip room drawn into the pattern rather than into a second size run. The trade-off is worth stating plainly: a unisex cut suits a wide range of people well and a narrow range of people perfectly. If your chest and hip measurements sit a long way apart, write to us before you order.

Synthetics hold their measurements. Cotton and cotton blends can lose up to 3% in length on the first wash, and the patterns are cut with that allowance already in them. Merino will shrink and felt if it meets warm water, so cold wool cycle only, laid flat to dry. Each product page states what that cloth does.

Ordering and shipping

Standard shipping is free on orders over $125, and a flat $8 below that. Expedited is $18 flat, whatever the order is worth. Alaska, Hawaii and the US territories travel USPS Priority in three to seven business days. The whole table is on the shipping page.

Within one business day of the order, from our own warehouse rather than a third party. Anything placed after 1:00 p.m. Eastern on a Friday goes out on Monday. The tracking number is emailed the moment the label is printed.

To Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia and New Zealand. Rates are worked out at checkout by weight and destination. Duties and import taxes are paid by the recipient on delivery, and we cannot quote them in advance, because they turn on your country and on the classification your customs authority applies.

Yes, as long as it has not shipped. Send the order number and we will change the size, change the address, or cancel it outright. Once a tracking number exists the parcel belongs to the carrier, and the quickest route is to take delivery and start a return.

Tell us and we will settle it rather than sending you off to the carrier. Most of these turn up inside 48 hours, usually with a neighbour or around a side door. If yours has not appeared after two business days we send a replacement and carry the carrier claim ourselves.

Returns, exchanges and repairs

Thirty days from delivery, unworn, unwashed and with the tags still on. Start it from the returns page with your order number and the email address on the order.

Size exchanges: we do, both directions, once per order. Refund returns: a $7 label fee comes out of the refund. Faulty pieces, or anything we sent in error: we pay, always, and no fee applies.

The fee exists because freight is a real cost and it has to sit somewhere. Folding it into every price would mean the people who keep what they buy quietly paying for the people who do not.

You can exchange it for a different size within 30 days, but Slow Line pieces cannot be refunded, because the cloth was cut for your order and no one else's. That is stated on the product page before you buy, and you can change the size at no cost within 48 hours of ordering.

If it is a fault in materials or construction, yes: repaired or replaced free, with no time limit on it. Honest wear is a different matter. Zip replacement, patching and similar work is charged at parts cost plus return shipping, usually $18 to $45, with a two to three week turnaround from the Asheville workshop. Send a photograph first and we will quote before you post anything.

We process refunds within two business days of the parcel arriving. Your bank then takes three to ten business days to post it, which is outside anyone's hands here. We email you the moment we process it, so you hold a timestamp if you need to chase your card issuer.

Yes, and gladly. Frayed cuffs, worn hems, failed zips, lost pulls, torn pockets and elbows gone thin are everyday work at the bench. Send a photograph to help@haldenfield.com and we quote before you post anything, so nothing travels on a guess.

Wear repairs run about $18 to $45 in parts plus return shipping, and come back in two to three weeks. A fault in the making is separate and costs nothing, at any age. We would rather patch a jacket you already trust than sell you the replacement for it.

Volumes, care and the company

There is no sale calendar, no flash sale, and no discount code handed out to bring new customers in. Prices are set on landed cost plus a fixed margin, and then held. Repaired and returned pieces do go out at a lower price through the resale program, and there is a referral credit, which is the nearest thing to a discount here.

Volume 07 opens on October 2, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. Eastern. Volume 06 released on March 6, 2026 and is still on the shelf in most sizes. We publish a date once it is certain, and we run no countdown clocks and no early access tiers.

A volume is a small release that sits on top of the standing line, and there are two a year. Twenty-two of our thirty styles are seasonless and stay on the shelf the whole year round. A volume adds the rest: a handful of pieces cut in a color or a cloth that suits one stretch of weather, and given a name rather than a season.

Volume 06: Longshadow released on March 6, 2026. Volume 07 opens on October 2, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. Eastern. When a volume sells through it is finished, and we do not reissue it, though a cloth or a pattern that earns its keep usually carries into the standing line. Shops order against a volume eight weeks before it opens.

Cold, gentle, inside out, and never with fabric softener. Softener coats the fiber and takes the wicking and the water repellency with it. A waterproof shell wants a technical wash and then twenty minutes on a low tumble, which wakes the repellent finish back up rather than replacing it.

Every product page carries instructions for that particular cloth, and they are worth reading rather than generalising from one garment to the next.

Cold wool cycle, a wool detergent, and lay it flat to dry out of direct sun. Warm water and a tumble dryer will shrink it and felt it, and neither can be undone afterwards. Beyond that, merino asks to be washed less often than most people wash it. Hang a layer overnight by an open window and it comes back fresh.

Pilling under a pack strap or a seatbelt is friction, not a fault. A wool comb or a fabric shaver lifts it off and the piece carries on. If a hole opens at the elbow, send it to the workshop rather than to the bin.

Gift cards never expire and carry no fees, from $25 to $500, delivered by email. The referral credit takes $20 off your friend's first order and puts $20 of credit in your account once that order ships. The whole of the terms sits on the gift cards page.

Possibly. We work with independent physical shops, on a $2,500 opening order at keystone margin, with terms published rather than negotiated one at a time. We do not supply online-only resellers or marketplaces. Apply from the stockists page.
