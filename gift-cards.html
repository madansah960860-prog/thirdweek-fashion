Gift cards and referrals

Two ways to put Halden Field in someone else's hands without having to guess their chest measurement. Both sets of terms are short enough to read standing up, and neither one hides a date in a footnote at the bottom.

Digital gift cards

Sent by email, usually inside a minute, and you can hold delivery for a particular morning if the occasion has a date on it. A card spends against anything on this site, Slow Line pieces included.

What a card does

  • Amounts: $25, $50, $100, $150, $250 or $500
  • Expiry: None, ever. No expiry date, and no dormancy or inactivity fee waiting to eat it
  • Balance: Spend part of it and the rest simply stays there until it is wanted
  • Combining: Works alongside referral credit, and alongside another card
  • Refunds: A card itself cannot be refunded. Anything bought with one follows the ordinary 30-day return policy
  • Lost card: Send us the purchase details and we will write it out again

The payment link comes here, and so does the note confirming the card arrived.

Referral credit

Your friend takes $20 off a first order. You get $20 in credit once that order has shipped and cleared the return window. That is the whole arrangement, and there is nothing on the far side of it.

How it runs

  • Pass your code to someone who has not bought from us before
  • They type it in at checkout and $20 comes off an order of $100 or more
  • Once that order ships and the 30-day window closes, $20 in credit appears against your account
  • It comes off by itself at your next checkout. Nothing to remember and nothing to paste in

The rules, all of them

  • New customers only, and one referral discount to a person
  • The friend's order has to reach $100 for the discount to apply
  • No ceiling on how many people you pass the code to
  • Credit expires 12 months after it is issued, and we email you before that date rather than after it
  • It cannot be turned into cash
  • Where a referral is plainly to yourself we may hold the credit back, and we will write and say why rather than quietly voiding it

What it is not

  • Not an affiliate scheme. No commission on sales, no tiers, no leaderboard
  • Not a paid arrangement with anyone, whatever their following
  • The link drops no tracking pixel on the person you send it to
  • We do not write to anyone you refer unless they sign up themselves

Finding your code

A code is made with your first order and sits in the shipping confirmation email. This is only the shape of one, so you know what you are looking for further down that message.

Yours will read differently and is tied to the email address on your order.
