What these notes are for

Most brand journals exist to catch search traffic. This one exists because the same four questions keep arriving in our support inbox, and a product page is a poor place to answer any of them at length.

We publish when there is something worth saying, which comes out at roughly one piece every six weeks. They are written at the field desk by our product developers and our two coaches, and edited for accuracy before style. Where a piece recommends something we make, it says so in plain sight, and it says just as plainly when something we sell is the wrong choice for you.

How we work

What we write about

  • Weather: what to wear at what temperature, and how much of it to take off at mile two
  • Materials: what a fabric claim means, held up against what the mill can actually document
  • Seasons: how a twelve-week block is shaped, and the ordinary obstacles that end most of them early
  • Repair: getting a few more winters out of something you already own
  • What we got wrong: decisions we reversed, and what they cost to reverse

A note when we publish

One email when a new piece goes up, plus the dates the volumes open. Nothing daily, and no sequence that follows you around afterwards.
