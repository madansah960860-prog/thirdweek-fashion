What a volume is

A volume is a small, dated run of pieces that sit outside the core line. We cut a fixed quantity, publish the date well ahead of it, and then stop. Sizes empty at their own pace, so a volume is usually part-available for months rather than gone in an hour.

The date is simply the date. If you miss it, most volume fabrics find their way into a core style within a year, which is where they were always headed.
