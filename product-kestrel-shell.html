Kestrel Shell Jacket

$268

In the Blue Ridge, rain is a season rather than an event. This is our lightest fully waterproof shell, made for the months when waiting for the weather to pass would cost you the winter. The membrane is rated to 20,000mm and every seam is taped, and the jacket still packs into its own chest pocket at roughly the size of a water bottle.

A kestrel holds still in moving air, which is close to what we asked this jacket to do. The back hem drops two inches below the front so it stays put over a belt when you lean into a climb, and the hood turns with your head instead of sliding across your face.

Fabric

One cloth, one membrane, no bonded panels to lift apart in the third season.

  • Shell: 100% recycled ripstop nylon, 40 denier, bluesign approved mill
  • Membrane: PFAS-free polyurethane, 20,000mm water column, 15,000g/m2/24h breathability
  • Seams: Fully taped with 13mm seam tape
  • Finish: PFAS-free durable water repellent, renewable in a low tumble dry
  • Weight: 8.4 oz (238 g) in men's medium

Fit

Cut to sit over the Emberline Grid Crew without pulling across the back.

  • Cut: Athletic regular. Fits over a base layer and a mid layer without pulling
  • Length: Dropped back hem, 2 in longer than the front
  • Sleeve: Articulated elbow, elastic bound cuff, no velcro tab
  • Hood: Adjustable at the rear, brim holds shape in wind
  • On the model: 6 ft 1 in tall, 40 in chest, wearing size M over a mid layer

Care

Washing this jacket properly is most of what keeps it waterproof. Grime held in the face fabric is what usually stops water beading, not a failed membrane.

  • Wash: Machine wash cold, gentle cycle, technical wash, zips closed
  • Dry: Tumble low for 20 minutes to reactivate the water repellent finish
  • Avoid: Fabric softener, bleach, dry cleaning, direct iron heat
  • Reproof: A spray-on treatment once a season, from the point water stops beading on the shoulders

Sizing

Unisex sizing, measured in inches. If you land between two sizes, the note below is the call we would make.

  • Range: XS to XXL, unisex cut with US numeric equivalents in the size guide
  • Between sizes: The smaller size if you wear it over a tee, the larger if it lives over a fleece all winter
  • Chest for size M: Fits 38 to 41 in body chest
  • Full chart: See the size guide in inches

Before it entered the line

Two wet seasons of notes

No piece reaches a volume until it has come back from real weather with comments written on it. The Kestrel went out in late autumn and came back in spring, which is the only honest way to test a rain shell. The numbers below are the floor for everything we make, not a claim we save for the expensive jacket.

8Weeks on test
12Runners wearing it
50Wash cycles minimum
20,000Millimetre water column

What the testers changed

  • The hood brim was rebuilt after the first block, because the original folded flat in a headwind
  • The chest pocket zip moved inboard so it no longer sits under a pack strap
  • The cuff lost its adjuster tab, which three testers said caught on a watch

What we retest after 50 washes

  • Water column, measured on washed panels rather than new cloth
  • Seam tape adhesion at the shoulder and the underarm, where it is worked hardest
  • Whether water still beads, and how far a low tumble dry brings the finish back

How a piece gets made here

The rest of the detail

  • Two-way front zip with an internal storm flap and a chin garage
  • One chest pocket, which doubles as the stuff sack. The jacket packs to roughly 8 in by 4 in
  • Laser-cut back vent under a bonded storm flap
  • Reflective trim at the rear hem and both cuffs
  • No internal hanging loop. It adds a hard point that works through the membrane from the inside

A 20,000mm rating means a column of water 20,000 millimeters tall can stand on the fabric before it passes through. In practice, anything above 10,000mm holds up to a full day of rain. The number that matters more over a long wet hour is breathability: at 15,000g this shell moves enough vapor that you stay dry from the inside on all but the warmest wet days.

If you mostly walk or commute in rain rather than run in it, the Rainshadow 2.5L is the better jacket. It is heavier, longer and vents through pit zips.

Full shipping detail

We repair tears and failed zips on this jacket for as long as we can source the parts, at cost plus shipping. A patched shell is a jacket with a history, not a jacket that failed. When it is genuinely finished, send it back and we will route it into textile recycling instead of landfill. The full process sits on the sustainability page.
