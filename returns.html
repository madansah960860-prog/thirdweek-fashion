Returns and exchanges

Thirty days from delivery, unworn with the tags still on. Size exchanges travel free both ways. Refund returns carry a $7 label fee. That is the entire policy, and everything below is simply how it works and why it is drawn that way. Nothing on this page has a clause hiding under it.

The $7 label fee on refund returns is there because freight is a real cost and it has to sit somewhere. Folding it into every price would mean the people who keep what they buy quietly paying for the people who send it back. Charging it at the point of return is the plainer arrangement of the two, and it is the reason our prices are not padded to cover it.

How to start a return

  1. Tell us what you have

    Use the form on this page, or write to help@haldenfield.com with the order number, the piece, and whether you want another size or your money back. There is no account to make and no password to remember.

  2. A prepaid label comes back

    Usually within a few hours, and always within one business day. It arrives as a UPS or USPS label you can print at home, or as a code you show at a drop-off counter if there is no printer in the house.

  3. Pack it and hand it over

    Use our mailer again if it came through the journey intact. The tags need to still be on, and the piece needs to be unworn and unwashed. There is no slip to fill in and nothing to put in the box beside the garment.

  4. We look at it and act

    Within two business days of the parcel landing. On an exchange, the new size goes out the same day the old one arrives, because we set that size aside in your name the moment you asked. It cannot sell out while your parcel is on the road.

  5. The money goes back

    To the card or account it came from. We process it within two business days, then your bank takes three to ten business days to post it, which is the slow part and not ours to hurry. We email you the moment we send it.

What can come back, and what cannot

Yes

  • Anything unworn and unwashed with the tags on, inside 30 days of delivery
  • Anything tried on indoors. You have to put a jacket on to know whether it is yours
  • Gifts, sent back by whoever received them, for store credit at the price paid
  • Orders paid for with referral credit, refunded back in the same proportions they were paid
  • Anything that has failed, at any age, with no time limit, under our repair terms

No

  • Anything worn outdoors or put through a wash, because we cannot sell it to anyone else
  • Socks and headbands once the hygiene seal or the packaging is open
  • Slow Line pieces, for a refund. Size exchange only, because the cloth was cut for you
  • Gift cards, which cannot be refunded, though they also never expire
  • Resale program pieces, which are final sale and labeled that way before you buy

When something has failed

  • A fault in the making is repaired or replaced at no cost, with no limit on how old the piece is
  • A fault means materials or construction giving way: a seam that opens, a zip that quits under ordinary use
  • Honest wear is not a fault, but the bench will still quote for it, usually $18 to $45
  • Send a photograph first. We quote before you post anything anywhere

When the mistake is ours

  • Wrong size, wrong piece, something missing, or damage done on the way
  • We pay the postage in both directions and no fee applies
  • The replacement leaves before the first parcel has found its way back
  • No photographs asked for, and no claim form to sign

Order two sizes if you cannot decide

Buying two sizes and sending one back is a fair use of this policy, and we would rather you did that than guessed and lived with it. Free US shipping applies over $125, and one size exchange per order travels free both ways. If neither one stays, the $7 label fee is charged once, not twice.

The other option

Repairs instead of returns

A return is the right answer when a piece was never going to fit you. It is the wrong answer when a piece fits perfectly and one part of it has given up. Most of what comes back to the workshop is not worn out, it is worn in one place, and that is an afternoon of work rather than a new jacket.

What the bench takes on

  • Zips replaced, pulls and sliders refitted
  • Seams restitched, hems and cuffs rebuilt
  • Holes and tears patched in matching cloth where we still hold it
  • Elastic, drawcords and snaps renewed
  • Water repellency refreshed on shells that have stopped beading

What it costs

  • A fault in materials or construction is repaired or replaced at no cost, with no time limit
  • Honest wear is charged at parts cost plus return shipping, typically $18 to $45
  • Two to three weeks on the bench in Asheville, and we tell you the week it will be back
  • We quote first. Nothing is repaired and billed without your word on it
  • There is no charge for looking at a piece and telling you it is not worth mending

How to start one

  • Photograph the damage in daylight, close enough to see the stitch
  • Send it to help@haldenfield.com with the piece and its age
  • We reply with a price and an address inside one business day
  • Post it when you are ready. There is no window to miss and no form to complete
  • It comes back cleaned, mended, and carrying the patina it earned

Still inside the thirty days

If a piece arrived faulty, do not send it to the bench. Start a return instead and we will replace it outright at no cost, with the postage on us in both directions. Repairs are for the years afterwards, not for the first month.
