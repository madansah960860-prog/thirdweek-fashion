Shipping and delivery

Every order is picked and packed in our own building in Asheville, North Carolina. Nothing is drop-shipped and nothing passes through a third party, so when you write to us about a parcel we can walk over and look at the shelf it came off. The rates below are the whole of it, with no handling charge waiting at checkout.

When it leaves the building

  • Ordered before 1:00 p.m. Eastern on a business day, and it goes out that afternoon
  • Everything after that goes out the next business day
  • Nothing ships on weekends or on US federal holidays
  • Slow Line pieces are cut and sewn to order, so they leave after an 18 to 24 business day window

Following it

  • The tracking number is emailed the moment the label is printed
  • A number can sit still for 24 hours before the first scan, which is normal and not a problem
  • An order that travels in two parcels is never charged twice, and each parcel is tracked on its own
  • No account and no password are needed to follow an order
Free shipping threshold$125

The threshold sits at roughly what an average parcel costs us to send. It is not a number chosen to nudge a second item into your bag.

International

We post to five destinations beyond the United States. Rates are worked out at checkout by weight and by where the parcel is going, rather than averaged into every price so that domestic orders quietly pay for them.

What we will not do at the border

  • We cannot quote your duties. They turn on your country and on how your customs authority classifies the goods, and a guess from us would only mislead you
  • We will not mark a parcel as a gift or write down its value. That is customs fraud, and we are not going to do it on your behalf
  • We cannot refund duties on a returned piece. Your customs authority may let you reclaim them, and we will send whatever paperwork supports the claim
  • We do not pay for returns from abroad. Return shipping from outside the United States is at your cost

When a parcel goes wrong

Marked delivered, nothing there

  • Try side doors, back porches and neighbours first. Most turn up inside 48 hours
  • If it has still not appeared after two business days, write to us
  • We send a replacement and take the carrier claim on ourselves
  • No police report, no affidavit, no statement to sign

Damaged on the way

  • Send a photograph of the outer parcel and of the piece inside
  • The replacement leaves the same day or the next business day
  • The damaged piece does not have to come back first, and often we ask you to keep it
  • Nothing to pay, in any circumstance

Wrong piece, or wrong size

  • Our mistake, so we pay the postage in both directions
  • The right size is set aside in your name while the wrong one travels back
  • Write to us and a prepaid label comes the same day
  • If you would rather not wait at all, a refund is processed within two business days

A wrong address

Write to us before the parcel ships and we will change it at no cost. Once a tracking number exists the carrier holds it, and re-routing after that is a coin toss rather than a service. If you notice the error in the minutes after ordering, tell us then rather than waiting to see what happens.

What turns up at the door

Packaging, plainly

A parcel from us is meant to be unremarkable. Paper, a label, the piece you ordered, and nothing else asking for your attention. Here is what that means in practice, including the part we have not solved yet.

What leaves here

  • Paper mailers and paper cartons, both recyclable and both made from recycled content
  • No plastic polybags in anything we send out
  • A carton only when the shape of the piece needs one. Soft layers travel folded in a mailer
  • No printed catalog, no coupon card, no tissue paper with a slogan on it

What we have not solved

  • Stock still arrives from our mills and factories in polybags, one garment to a bag
  • We reuse those bags inside the building where we can, and we are working the rest of the way up the chain
  • Saying we are plastic free would be easier than this paragraph, and it would not be true
  • Progress on it is written up each year in sustainability and materials

If you want something in the box

We will leave a gift note, and shops can ask for a line sheet. Beyond that, the box stays empty of paper you did not ask for. Tell us at checkout or write to us before the order ships.
