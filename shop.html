The whole line, 30 styles

Twenty-two seasonless core pieces plus the current volume, all in one grid. Each style carries its fabric composition, fit notes and care instructions on its own page. Nothing here is a placeholder, and nothing is on a timer.

How the line is organised

Three kinds of thing, and that is all

Core

Twenty-two styles that stay in stock all year and are restocked rather than redesigned. If a core piece fits you now, it will fit the same way next spring. Look for the Core tag.

Volume

Two dated runs a year, made in a fixed quantity and then stopped. Volume 06, Longshadow, released March 6, 2026. Sizes sell through at their own pace, so a volume is usually part-available for months.

Slow Line

Cut in Asheville after you order, one garment at a time, with an 18 to 24 business day lead time. Slow Line pieces can be exchanged for another size but are not eligible for refund.

Ask us about fit

Buying from us, plainly

Halden Field sells direct to you in US dollars. There is no wholesale layer inside the price and no markdown calendar, so the number you read in September is the number you read in March.

Twenty-two of the thirty styles here are core pieces that get restocked rather than replaced. The rest belong to a dated volume. Volume 06 released on March 6, 2026, and Volume 07 opens on October 2, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. Eastern.

More about how it is made

What every product page tells you

  • Full fabric composition by percentage, trims and thread included
  • Garment weight in ounces, fabric weight in gsm
  • Fit notes, plus the model's height and the size they are wearing
  • Care written for the actual fabric, not copied from a generic laundry label
  • Body measurements in inches, cross-referenced to the size guide
