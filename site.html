Years, not seasons

How long a jacket lasts is decided at the fabric table.

We started Halden Field in 2019 because good kit kept giving out in the same four places: the zip garage, the underarm seam, the waistband elastic and the print. So we work in the opposite order. Fabric first, seam construction second, fit third, graphics last, and only if the graphic is still legible after fifty washes.

Every garment gets a wear-test block before it goes on sale: eight weeks, twelve athletes, weather from Asheville rain to high desert heat. If a sample comes back with a failure, the run does not open. That is why the line moves slowly, and why the core does not change with the season.

The field standard

Cut once, mended twice

Three rules hold for every piece we make, from the $42 cap to the $340 puffer. They are the reason the line stops at 30 styles, and the reason a Halden Field garment is worth keeping past the first winter.

One fabric lot per style

  • Each style is woven in a single lot, so a restock matches the first run in color and in hand
  • The mill and the country of weaving are named on the product page, not hidden behind a trade name
  • Nothing goes on sale until it holds its shape within 3 percent after fifty wash cycles

Seams a tailor can open

  • Flat-felled and bar-tacked at the four points that give out first: zip garage, underarm, waistband, hem
  • Every style is cut with 5/8 in of seam allowance, so there is cloth left to take in or let out
  • Thread sits one shade darker than the shell, which makes an honest repair easy to match later

Repair before replacement

  • Send a worn piece back and we repair zips, seams and cuffs at cost, for as long as we hold the fabric
  • We keep spare trims for every core style: zip pulls, cord locks, elastic and drawcord
  • If a garment is past saving we take it back for material recovery rather than leave that to you

Materials, repair and what we take back

One piece, up close

Kestrel Shell Jacket

$268

A 2.5-layer shell with a 20,000mm water column and taped seams, cut long at the back so it stays where you put it over a pack. It folds into its own chest pocket, about the size of a water bottle, and can live there for the rest of the day.

Fabric

  • Shell: 100% recycled ripstop nylon, 40 denier, bluesign approved
  • Membrane: PFAS-free polyurethane, 20,000mm water column, 15,000g breathability
  • Weight: 8.4 oz in a men's medium
  • Finish: PFAS-free durable water repellent, renewable with low heat tumble

Fit

  • Cut: Regular through the chest, with room for a mid layer and no loose cloth to catch wind
  • Length: Dropped back hem, 2 in longer than the front
  • Sleeve: Articulated elbow, elastic-bound cuff
  • Model: 6 ft 1 in, chest 40 in, wearing size medium

Care and sizing

  • Wash: Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle with technical wash
  • Dry: Tumble low for 20 minutes, which brings the water repellent back to life
  • Avoid: Fabric softener, bleach, dry cleaning, direct iron heat
  • Sizing: XS to XXL. Between sizes, take the smaller for a close fit and the larger if you layer underneath

How Halden Field works

We sell direct, in US dollars, from our own site. No wholesale layer inside the price, no markdown calendar, and no urgency we invented to move stock.

A seasonless core, two dated volumes

  • Twenty-two core styles stay in stock all year and get restocked rather than redesigned
  • Two volumes a year, cut in small runs. Volume 06 released on March 6, 2026
  • Volume 07 opens on October 2, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. Eastern
  • When a volume is gone we mark it sold through instead of leaving a dead listing up

Getting it to you, and back

  • Free US standard shipping over $125, a flat $8 below that
  • Two to five business days anywhere in the contiguous US, sent from Asheville, North Carolina
  • 30 days to return or exchange, unworn with the tags still on
  • Exchange shipping is on us. A refund return carries a $7 label fee

The Slow Line, timed honestly

  • Slow Line pieces are cut in Asheville after you order, one at a time
  • The lead time is 18 to 24 business days, printed on every Slow Line product page
  • You are charged when you order, because the cloth is cut for you and cannot go back
  • Slow Line pieces can be exchanged for another size but are not eligible for refund

Gift cards, referrals, stockists

  • Digital gift cards from $25 to $500, with no expiry and nothing taken out of the balance in fees
  • Send a friend $20 off their first order and we credit you $20 once it ships
  • Shops that want to carry the line can apply through our stockist page
  • Wholesale minimums, margins and lead times are published, not negotiated case by case
