Wholesale, printed in the open

Our terms are published rather than negotiated one shop at a time. The shops we want to work with are small, and the person doing the buying is usually also the person on the floor at four o'clock. They do not have three weeks to spend on a conversation about margin. Read the numbers below. If they work, the rest is a line sheet and a date.

The numbers

  • Opening order: $2,500 at wholesale, mixed across whichever styles suit your floor
  • Reorders: $750
  • Margin: Keystone, 50% off the published retail price
  • Payment: Net 30 from the second order onward, prepaid on the first
  • Lead time: 10 to 15 business days from the purchase order on standing styles
  • Volumes: Allocation for a volume closes eight weeks before its release date

What we ask of you

  • A room with a door and a rail. We do not supply online-only resellers or marketplaces
  • Our published US price held on the shelf, so nobody is charged extra for shopping near home
  • Staff who will learn the cloth. We send a one-page sheet and samples to wear
  • Nothing listed on third-party marketplace platforms

What you get back

  • Free freight on anything above the reorder minimum
  • Two chances a year to swap out standing stock that has not moved
  • Damaged or faulty stock replaced on your word, with no claim form in between
  • Your shop named on this page, and named again when we send a local customer your way

Ask about carrying the line

Fill this in and a reply comes within two business days, with a line sheet and a wholesale price list attached. There is no application fee, no territory to buy and no exclusivity asked for in either direction.

We call about this application and nothing else.

Thirty characters minimum. One honest paragraph tells us more than a deck.
